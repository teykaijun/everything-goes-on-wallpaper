Everything Goes On — Arena & Chibi Lux | Automatic Day / Night

Bring a little Star Guardian magic to your desktop. This landscape wallpaper pairs the Everything Goes On TFT arena with an interactive Chibi Lux: a warm classroom by day, a glowing cosmic battlefield by night.

Twenty small desks keep their own belongings and personality. Lux walks around the available floor, visits scenery, reacts to your cursor and performs native emotes. Click accessible floor space to guide her, click Lux for an emote, or use the bottom-right star menu for Dance, Laugh, Taunt, Joke and Rest.

Features
• Automatic local-time day/night switching (07:00 / 19:00 by default).
• Adjustable theme, schedule, crossfade, music volume and Lux size/speed.
• 2560×1440, 30 fps arena loops, upscaled from 1080p source footage.
• Local media playback; no online streaming required.
• Bench bands reserved for desktop icons. Icons are not moved automatically.
• Resize settling and Lux graphics-context recovery for monitor changes.

Use the matching Star Window project for a portrait second monitor. Choose independent wallpapers per display. Start with 30 fps or lower on modest hardware; performance depends on your GPU and wallpaper-host settings.
